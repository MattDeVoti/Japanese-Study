Privacy Policy — Omedetou

Last updated: 5 August 2026

Omedetou is a Japanese study app. This policy explains, in plain terms, what the app does and does not do with your information.

Omedetou does not collect anything about you. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no analytics, no advertising, no trackers and no third-party SDKs. Your study progress never leaves your device unless you choose to send it somewhere.

There is exactly one thing you can choose to send, and it is described below.

What stays on your device

Everything you do in the app is stored on your device and nowhere else:

  • Your study progress, review schedule, test results and grades
  • Flashcard checkmarks, favourites and “needs work” marks
  • Custom lessons you build
  • Settings: appearance, text size, audio and reminder preferences
  • Game unlocks and high scores

None of it is transmitted, and the developer has no way to see any of it.

Optional iCloud sync

If you turn on iCloud sync, your progress is copied to your own private iCloud account so you can continue on another of your devices. This is Apple’s private database — the developer cannot read it, and it is governed by Apple’s privacy policy. You can turn it off at any time.

Send Feedback — the one thing that is sent

The app has a Send Feedback screen. If, and only if, you write a message there and tap Submit, the following is sent to the developer:

  • The text you wrote.
  • The app version and your iOS version — so a reported bug can be traced to the right build.

That is the complete list. Nothing is attached that identifies you or your device: no name, no email address, no account, no device identifier, no location, and none of your study data. Because no contact details are collected, feedback cannot be replied to.

The message is delivered through a third-party form-relay service, which passes it to the developer’s email and does not publish it. Please do not include personal details in the message — there is a reminder to that effect on the screen itself.

If you never open that screen, the app makes no network requests at all.

Where your data can live, at a glance

Settings and unlocksYour device, and your own iCloud if sync is onNo
Feedback you type and submitThe developer’s email, via a form relayYes — that is its purpose

Children

The app is suitable for all ages. It collects no personal information, so it collects none from children either.

Data retention and deletion

Because nothing is collected, there is nothing to request or delete. Feedback messages you choose to send are kept in the developer’s email only as long as needed to act on them. Deleting the app removes all of your study data from the device permanently — use Backup & Restore first if you want to keep it.

Changes

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here and the date above will change.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent through the Send Feedback screen in the app.
